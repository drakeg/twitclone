# Commercialization hypothesis

Ripple is being evaluated as a potential paid product for small clubs,
associations, nonprofits, and membership communities. This document separates
market hypotheses from validated customer evidence.

## Target buyer hypothesis

Small organizations whose staff or volunteer administrators currently coordinate
members through spreadsheets, email lists, social groups, or general-purpose
membership tools and want a branded community with less manual administration.

This is not validated demand yet.

## Pain hypothesis

Potential operator pain to validate:

- member and role information spread across multiple tools;
- repetitive moderation and member-management work;
- little visibility into recent membership/community activity;
- community platforms priced above what a small organization wants to pay;
- fragmented discussion, messaging, and knowledge resources.

## Existing Ripple leverage

The first pilot intentionally reuses current capabilities:

- persistent Spaces with owner/moderator/member roles;
- member profiles and messaging;
- community posts and durable resources;
- local moderation, appeals, and audit history;
- notifications and analytics foundations;
- Docker Compose self-hosting and the existing low-cost production path.

No separate tenant system, external SaaS dependency, or new payment processor is
required for the first validation slice.

## Competitive context checked 2026-11-19

- Circle Professional was listed at $89/month:
  https://circle.so/pricing
- Bettermode Starter was listed at $149/month:
  https://bettermode.com/pricing
- Software Advice reported that membership-management buyers frequently mention
  manual spreadsheet tracking and fragmented member/payment data, and that many
  buyers in its advisor-call sample were quoted roughly $100–$350/month:
  https://www.softwareadvice.com/membership-management/

These references provide market context only. They do not establish demand for
Ripple or willingness to pay.

## Pilot feature

**Community Operator Dashboard**

For a Space owner or moderator, provide a read-only operating view showing:

- total members;
- owner/moderator count;
- new members in the last 30 days;
- visible posts and linked resources in the last 30 days;
- pending moderation appeals;
- moderation actions in the last 30 days;
- recent members;
- direct links to existing role, conversation, resource, and moderation tools.

Metrics are descriptive counts derived from existing records. They must not be
presented as engagement quality, reputation, rankings, or causal analytics.

## Pricing hypothesis

Test a hosted pilot in the range of **$49–$79/month** for a small organization.
A setup/migration fee may be tested only when real onboarding work justifies it.

Do not implement new billing behavior until real prospect/customer evidence
supports the offer.

## Validation questions

Ask real prospects:

1. How do you currently manage member roles, conversations, moderation, and
   member updates?
2. Which tasks require spreadsheets, copy/paste, or switching between tools?
3. How many staff/volunteer hours per month does that work consume?
4. Which operator summary would save the most time each week?
5. What are you paying today for community/membership tools?
6. Would a $49–$79/month hosted pilot be worth evaluating if it replaced a
   specific manual workflow?
7. What would prevent a pilot: migration effort, trust/security, missing
   features, training, integrations, or price?

## Success criteria

Product-market fit is not claimed until at least one real prospect confirms a
current painful workflow and expresses concrete pilot/payment interest. Prefer a
paid pilot over feature expansion that lacks customer evidence.
