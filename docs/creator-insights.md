# Creator Pro performance insights

Creator Pro turns Ripple's measured analytics into descriptive, actionable observations without claiming causation.

## What the insight layer uses

- daily-unique post impressions collected by Ripple
- reposts and non-removed quotes during the selected 7, 30, or 90-day range
- post publication dates for posting-cadence summaries
- hashtags already present in authored posts

## What Ripple may say

Ripple may identify:

- top measured posts by engagement rate
- posts performing above the account's own measured average
- top measured hashtags
- approximate posts-per-week and active publishing days
- limited-data conditions where stronger conclusions are not warranted

## What Ripple does not claim

The dashboard does not say a hashtag, posting frequency, or content style *caused* better performance. It describes correlations in measured Ripple activity only.

Posts or hashtags with zero measured impressions are excluded from performance rankings. If there is insufficient measured traffic, Ripple explicitly says that reliable patterns are not available yet.
