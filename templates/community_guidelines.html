Ripple Community Standards

Be part of a community people actually want to return to.
Our standard

Be human. Be respectful. Disagree without being cruel.

Ripple is meant to be a welcoming place for conversation, discovery, humor, disagreement, and different points of view. It is not a place for bullying, harassment, personal attacks, humiliation, intimidation, or deliberately making other people miserable.

You do not have to agree with everyone here. You do have to treat them like people.

What we expect

Disagree with ideas, not dignity

Challenge opinions, claims, and arguments without insulting, belittling, mocking, or demeaning the person behind them.

No bullying or harassment

Do not repeatedly target someone, encourage dogpiling, threaten, intimidate, stalk, or use mentions to provoke or harass them.

No hate or discrimination

Do not attack, demean, or exclude people because of race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sex, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or another protected characteristic.

Respect boundaries and privacy

Do not publish private personal information, sexualize or harass others, impersonate people deceptively, or keep contacting someone who has clearly asked you to stop.

No threats or encouragement of harm

Threats, celebration of real-world harm, or encouragement of violence against another person are not welcome here.

Don't ruin the room

Spam, scams, deliberate disruption, repetitive antagonism, and behavior whose main purpose is to annoy or derail other people may be moderated.

Reporting and moderation

If something crosses these lines, use the Report control on the content. Reports go to Ripple administrators for review. A report is a request for review—not an automatic finding that someone did something wrong.

Admins may dismiss a report or remove content that violates these standards. Context matters, and good-faith disagreement, criticism, satire, or an unpopular opinion is not automatically abuse.
